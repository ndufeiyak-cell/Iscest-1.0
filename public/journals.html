Publications

Journals

Three peer-reviewed journals covering comparative education, science and emerging technology, all published under ISCEST's editorial standards.

JCS

Journal of Computational Systems

Continuous publication. Covers distributed systems, algorithms, and computational theory. ISSN 2411-0091.

JAT

Journal of Applied Technology

Quarterly issues. Covers robotics, applied machine learning, and hardware-software systems. ISSN 2411-0108.

JSE

Journal of Sustainable Engineering

Biannual issues. Covers energy-efficient computing and green infrastructure research. ISSN 2411-0115.

Submission process

Manuscripts are submitted through the online portal, undergo double-blind peer review, and receive an editorial decision within 10–14 weeks on average.

Formatting templates and citation style guides are provided in the author guidelines.

Open access

All three journals are open access on publication. There is no reader-side paywall; article processing charges are waived for ISCEST members.

Archived issues remain freely available indefinitely.
